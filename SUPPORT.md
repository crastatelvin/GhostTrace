# Support

## How to get help

- **Documentation first**: `docs/quickstart.md` (build), `docs/cli.md` and
  `docs/gui-guide.md` (once shipped), and the design plan at
  `docs/plans/2026-10-23-ghosttrace-master-plan.md`.
- **Bugs and reproducible defects**: GitHub issues using the bug template.
- **Security matters**: private reporting per `SECURITY.md`.

## What we cannot help with

- **Uploading or inspecting real evidence.** Maintainers will never ask you to
  upload captured artifacts, case stores, or personal data, and cannot debug
  your real cases. Reports must be minimized to synthetic input or redacted
  public examples.
- **Forensic conclusions.** GhostTrace presents observations and uncertainty;
  it does not certify conclusions, custody, or legal admissibility.
- **Live acquisition guarantees.** Capturing a live host is best effort;
  protected copies and snapshots remain the investigator's responsibility
  (plan Section 6).

## Response expectations

This is a community project without paid staffing. Issues are triaged as
maintainer time allows; the tracker in the plan document shows what is
actually in progress. "Won't fix" answers will cite the plan (scope, phase,
or safety boundary) so you can see the reasoning.
