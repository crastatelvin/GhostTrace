# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a respectful, rigorous, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, or background. Forensic
work touches sensitive situations; the community around it holds itself to a
high standard of care.

## Our standards

**Expected behavior**

- Technical critique aimed at code and claims, not people.
- Evidence-first discussion: show what you observed, and state uncertainty
  plainly.
- Respect for privacy: never request, post, or forward real evidence or
  personal data.
- Patience with reviewers and reviewers alike; correctness over speed.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information, including case or artifact material.
- Demands for immediate support, or repeated derailing after a scope decision
  has been explained by citing the documented plan.
- Any use of this project to surveil people without legal authorization.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers via the repository's private channels
(Security → Report a vulnerability for anything security-sensitive, otherwise
open an issue asking for a private conversation with the maintainers).
Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban contributors
whose behavior violates this code, and will document the reason for the
record.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions,
chat) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
